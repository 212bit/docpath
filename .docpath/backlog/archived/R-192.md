## 背景

Quick-profile dogfood exposed workflow DX friction where DocPath still behaves like a one-size-fits-all heavy state machine or reports noisy secondary issues. The combined improvement backlog should address: start/go/step/current_step inconsistency; done re-running or failing to reuse fresh check/review evidence; volatile workspace markdown causing docs-index stale noise; pending-done becoming a primary route before all plan steps are complete; changes/commit not consuming its own canonical staging plan; install-manifest drift noise not distinguishing pre-existing baseline; review warnings not distinguishing new warnings from existing baseline; quick scope guard configurability for document-only allowances; and clearer profile/gate policy visibility in status/done output.

## 验收

Implemented fixes must reduce workflow friction without weakening standard gates. Acceptance: (1) start/go/step done route consistency is covered by tests; (2) done can reuse fresh gate evidence when safe and explains when it cannot; (3) docs-index stale checks ignore volatile workspace rendered markdown; (4) pending-done is secondary until all planned steps are done; (5) changes/commit can act on canonical evidence staging plans while excluding tasks/current aliases by default; (6) install/review drift output separates baseline/pre-existing noise from current-task regressions where practical; (7) quick scope guard has clearer configurable/document-only allowances; (8) status/done expose active workflow profile and gate policy clearly; (9) pnpm test, typecheck, docpath check/review/done pass or any remaining issue is explicitly documented.
